6 Vendor Guidelines

Please read the vendor guidelines carefully and acknowledge your agreement below.

  • This event is Saturday only.
  • Setup time will be announced once the entertainment schedule has been finalized.
  • Booth spaces may not exceed 16 feet.
  • No open flames are permitted.
  • There is one approved cooking vendor.
  • Merchandise must be family-friendly.
  • Vendors may take photos and short videos.
  • Vendors may NOT livestream the event.
  • Vendors posting on social media should tag @warthenrvpark.
  • Vendors must provide their own tent, tables, chairs, and display materials.
  • Vendors are responsible for cleaning their booth area.
  • The vendor fee is non-refundable once approved.
  • Warthen RV Park is not responsible for theft, damage, or lost property.
